New to PCL?

You only need a North American League account, Discord, and time for a full game.

What ranks can join?+

All skill levels are welcome.

How do I sign up?+

Join PCL through the signup-ticket invite, complete /signup, select your roles, and react to the active queue post.

Do I need a team?+

No. Join solo and the bot will place you in a balanced lobby.
