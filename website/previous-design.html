How do I join a game?

Join our Discord, complete /signup, and pick your primary and secondary roles. React to the active queue post to join. Extra players enter the waitlist.

What happens when the queue fills?

All ten players receive a ready check. Confirm within five minutes. Once everyone is ready, the bot generates balanced teams. Players who miss the check are removed and waiting players can take their spots.

How do ratings and results work?

PCL uses its own community ratings. Staff record completed game results in Discord, and season standings update on the website. These ratings are separate from your official League rank.

Does it cost anything to play?

No. Joining the community and playing our custom games is free.
